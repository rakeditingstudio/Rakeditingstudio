5-Step Reel Editing Checklist

A simple workflow to make your next short video clearer, smoother and more engaging.

FREE CREATOR GUIDE
01 · PLAN

Start with one idea

Decide what viewers should understand or feel. Keep one main message per Reel and choose a strong opening moment.

02 · CUT

Remove slow moments

Arrange your best clips first. Trim pauses, keep each shot purposeful and make the story easy to follow.

03 · SYNC

Match cuts to the beat

Use music you have permission to use. Place important transitions on clear beats, but avoid adding effects to every cut.

04 · POLISH

Keep text readable

Use high contrast, short captions and safe margins. Check that text is not hidden by app buttons or the caption area.

05 · EXPORT

Review before posting

Watch the full video once with sound and once muted. Check spelling, audio levels, framing and the final frame before export.
